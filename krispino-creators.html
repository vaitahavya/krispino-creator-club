Content guidelines

Keep it real

Do

  • Film your honest first-bite crunch & reaction.
  • Name the fusion clearly, e.g. "Guntur chilli × Jamaican jerk".
  • Keep the pack and red Krispino logo clearly visible.
  • Show chips in everyday moments: chai breaks, match nights, road trips.
  • Tag @krispino and add your personal discount code to the caption.

Don't

  • Make health or medical claims like "healthy" or "guilt-free".
  • Call Krispino a regional or single-state snack.
  • Show competing snack brands in the frame.
  • Post before your draft is approved in the Creator Dashboard.
  • Buy followers, likes, or views.

Disclosure is required under ASCI rules. Add #ad, #collab, or #KrispinoPartner at the start of your caption and on the video.
